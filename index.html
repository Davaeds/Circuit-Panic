How to Wire

  • Kill the power first. In this game, wires can't be touched while the breaker is on.
  • Black is hot, white is neutral. Red usually carries travelers between 3-way switches.
  • Hot goes to brass, neutral goes to silver. The silver screw feeds the socket shell, the part your fingers touch.
  • A switch breaks the hot, never the neutral. Otherwise the socket stays live even when the light is off.
  • On a 3-way, the dark screw is COMMON. The two brass screws are TRAVELERS.
  • Never let hot touch neutral directly. With nothing in between, that's a dead short: a bang, a flash and a tripped breaker.

In a puzzle: drag from one screw to another to run a wire (or click one screw, then another), then hook it clockwise. Two wires on one screw get pigtailed with a wire nut. Click a wire to cut it. B flips the breaker, S flips the switch, T picks up the neon tester.

This game simplifies things for learning. Your instructor and the NEC have the final word.
